About us

Learn by doing. Then do it again, faster.

Hackbridge was started by two Cambridge engineers who came back from an exchange at MIT wanting to bring its hacker culture home. Our purpose hasn't changed since: give members the skills and experience to push the boundaries of science and technology. Hands on, no red tape.
